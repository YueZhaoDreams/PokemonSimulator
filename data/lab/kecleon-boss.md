After the cap, the kept count is the one with the largest gain over the start under the 0-copy weights. If the start is higher, the start stays. Every other name still in the list is a cut. Nothing is protected. The step keeps the highest 0-copy weighted rate, then the higher worst active foe, then the cut that had more copies, then the name.
